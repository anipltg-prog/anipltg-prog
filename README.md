## Who we are

**Alpha Numeric Innovations** brings together renewable energy, electrical power infrastructure, professional lighting, automation and product innovation. Our focus is practical: build dependable systems that improve efficiency, visibility and everyday operation.

**AlphaNumeric Engineering & Technology** presents our lighting, automation and connected building solutions, with an emphasis on clear planning, careful integration and long-term usability.

Based in **Hyderabad, India**, our approach combines design, technology, implementation and support around the needs of each project.

## Connected building intelligence

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/connected-intelligence.png" alt="Light. Control. Intelligence. Professional lighting, smart automation and BMS/IoT integration." width="1200">
</p>

We connect building systems with the information people need to operate them: equipment status, energy use, alarms and historical trends.

- **Bring systems together:** HVAC, lighting, energy, water, access and other building services.
- **Extend existing investments:** add suitable IoT gateways and sensing around an installed BMS.
- **Make operations visible:** organize connected data into dashboards, alerts and site-level views.

Integration is planned around the installed equipment, available interfaces and project requirements.

## How we work

1. **Understand** — define the space, existing systems and intended outcomes.
2. **Design** — select compatible technology and plan the solution.
3. **Deliver** — coordinate implementation, integration, testing and commissioning.
4. **Support** — consider serviceability, ongoing operation and future expansion.
